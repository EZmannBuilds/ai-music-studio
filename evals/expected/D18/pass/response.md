## What is working, and why the second verse is weak

**Diagnosis first.** Verse 1 works because every line is an image: the ferry, the coat by the door,
the handwriting on the mailbox. Verse 2 repeats the ferry and the door and then switches to
statements ("I think about you often"), so it tells the listener what verse 1 already showed. The
rhyme scheme also collapses to one sound (-ore) for three lines, which makes it feel filled in
rather than written.

After the second chorus, the song has no turn: the narrator is in the same place at the end as at
the start.

## Options (nothing of yours has been changed)

**Option A: move time forward.** Keep your first line of verse 2 as a deliberate echo, then let the
other lines show a later season. You write the lines; the constraint is "no line may say what the
narrator feels".

**Option B: change the point of view.** Verse 2 as the person who left, seeing the same harbour.
Your chorus then reads differently the second time without a word of it changing.

**Option C: a short bridge instead of a third verse.** Four bars on the E chord, one line, and a
return to the chorus with the last line held. This answers "where does it go" without adding a
section you have to fill.
