# Human evaluation

The automatic checks in this run looked at structure and behaviour: whether a plan named its models,
whether a diagnosis stayed a diagnosis, whether a tradition was reduced to a scale. **They did not
judge the music, and nothing in this report does.** That is this sheet's job, and it stays with the
person who listened.

How to use it:

- Listen or read before looking at the run's REPORT.md, so the checks do not steer the judgement.
- Answer in words. A number invites averaging, and an average of "wonderful" and "unbearable" is
  "fine", which is neither answer.
- Where a check passed and the music still fails, say so under "Anything the checks missed?". That
  is the most useful line on the sheet, because it is how new cases get written.
- Blind A/B comparison against a strong direct prompt, as `research/BENCHMARK.md` describes, is worth
  the effort for any result you intend to rely on.
