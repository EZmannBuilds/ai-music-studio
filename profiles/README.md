# Profiles

The skills in this folder are the same for everyone. **What is yours goes in a profile.** That
means your paths, your DAW, your plugins and library editions, your analyzer, your default
deliverable, your folder layout, your naming preferences and your private terms.

1. Copy `user-profile.example.yaml` to `local/<your-name>.yaml`, or anywhere outside this folder.
2. Fill in what you know. Leave the rest empty, and the Music Director will ask when it needs a
   value.
3. Tell your agent runner where the profile is, for example in its instructions file.

`local/` is listed in `.gitignore`, so a profile kept there never reaches a repository.

The schema, and which specialist reads which key, is in `../shared/USER_PROFILE_SCHEMA.md`.
